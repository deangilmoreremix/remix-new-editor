Install the SmartVid Recorder

A free Chrome extension that records your screen, tab or camera and sends the recording straight to your SmartVid timeline.

Download SmartVid Recorder (.zip)
  1. Unzip the downloadRight-click the zip and extract it to a folder you'll keep, such as Documents/SmartVidRecorder. Don't delete this folder, because Chrome runs the extension from it.
  2. Open the extensions pageIn Chrome, Edge or Brave, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter (use edge://extensions in Edge).
  3. Turn on Developer modeUse the toggle in the top-right corner of the extensions page.
  4. Load the extensionClick Load unpacked and choose the folder you unzipped in step 1. The SmartVid Recorder appears in your list.
  5. Pin itClick the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to SmartVid Recorder so it's always one click away.
  6. RecordOpen the SmartVid editor, click the extension, choose screen, tab or camera and start recording. When you stop, click Send to SmartVid timeline in the panel that opens.
Chrome may show a "Disable developer mode extensions" message when it starts. Click Cancel or Keep to continue using the extension. This appears for any extension installed outside the Chrome Web Store.

Good to know

  • Works on desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera. It doesn't work on Safari, Firefox or phones.
  • Keep the SmartVid editor open in a tab when you click send. The extension opens it if it's closed.
  • Recordings stay on your computer until you send them.
  • To update, download the new zip, replace the contents of your folder, then click the refresh icon on the extension's card at chrome://extensions.

Troubleshooting

  • "Manifest file is missing": you selected the zip or the wrong folder. Pick the unzipped folder that contains manifest.json.
  • Nothing arrives in the editor: check that the editor tab is open and was loaded after you installed the extension. Reload it and send again.
  • Camera or mic blocked: allow access when Chrome asks, or turn it on under chrome://settings/content.
