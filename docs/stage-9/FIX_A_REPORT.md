## 1. GL transition window keeps each clip's own look (preview and export)

## 2. removeTransition is the inverse of addTransition

`src/cinegen/lib/editor/timeline-operations.ts`: a shared `transitionRippleSet` (clip B, every later clip on B's track, and the in-sync linked clips of each of them; slipped links excluded). `addTransition` shifts the set left by the overlap; `removeTransition` shifts it right by the overlap that is actually present (capped at the transition length; nothing moves when the clips no longer overlap). Behaviour change in `addTransition`: later clips on B's track now move with B (before they stayed, leaving a gap and shrinking/growing later overlaps).
Tests (`fixa-transitions.test.ts`, 5): add then remove `toEqual` the original timeline (incl. duration and linked audio), A-B-C chain with two transitions (C keeps its overlap, remove in either order restores exactly, C's audio stays synced), slipped audio keeps its offset, undo/redo by snapshots reproduces the same states, no-overlap remove moves nothing. Existing add-transition tests still pass. Limit: projects saved with the old non-rippling add have a gap after B; removing their transition shifts that tail too (the gap grows by the overlap).

## 3. Compound clip's own look

**Definition.** `flattenCompounds` / `expandCompound` distribute the compound's own look onto every picture child (audio children untouched), with the offset `child start - compound start` (`stage1/compound.ts`, `applyGroupLook`):
- `fx` and LUT: appended after the child's own (child fx, child LUT, then compound fx, compound LUT); fx param keys (`fx.keys` frames and `anim` `fx:i:p`) are shifted/re-indexed onto the appended effects.
- blend mode: used when the child is `normal`.
- pose (x, y, scale, rotation; keys, static transform, path, motion modifiers) and opacity (anim keys, legacy opacity keyframes, static): composed as compound-after-child (translate . rotate . scale) and sampled every 1/30 s (at most 600 samples) into the child's linear anim keys; the child's own modifiers/path are sampled in. A compound with only identity values or `fit` changes nothing (no baked keys). Because preview, export and expand all run this function, they agree, and expanding a compound keeps its look on the children (the look is baked on expand, the compound object is gone). Nested compounds work through the recursion.
**Evidence.** `fixa-compound-look.test.ts` (13 tests): continuity across the A|B cut, composition with child pose (group scale 2 doubles the child offset), opacity product without double counting, sway modifier uses compound time, fx order/offset/re-index, LUT/blend, audio untouched, real `exportClipsFromTimeline` + `exportItem`, expand equals flatten, row converter round trip, nested. Real export (log above): compound keyed x -30 to +30 % over 6 s plus Exposure +1.5; measured shift at 0.5/1.5/2.5/3.5/4.5/5.4 s = -320/-192/-64/64/192/308 px vs expected -320/-192/-64/64/192/307, continuous across the cut at 3 s; luma x1.23 to x1.65 vs x1.00 for the same timeline without the look (baseline shift 0 px). 3 assertions pass.
**Limits.** Applied per child, not as a group render: spatial effects (blur, glass) run per child, and group opacity multiplies each child (overlapping children on several tracks do not composite as one flattened group first). Rotation of the compound rotates children's offsets assuming a 16:9 frame (`GROUP_ASPECT`; exact when the compound is not rotated). Compound crop, fades, zoom, captions and volume keyframes are still not applied. Baked keys are not editable on the children (they are derived at flatten/expand time; the compound keeps the editable source).
