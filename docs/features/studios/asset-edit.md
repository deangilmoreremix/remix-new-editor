## What it does
In-browser canvas editor for campaign assets. Place headline, body and CTA text on a 9-position grid, and control size, color, background, font and alignment.

## Where in the app
Not in the side menu. Linked from Brand Studio, the Brand DNA editor and the campaign pages.

## Depends on
Campaign assets.
