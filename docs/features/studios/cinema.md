## What it does
Cinematic video and image generation with camera movement choices (dolly, crane, orbit, FPV drone) and film looks to set the mood. Video and Image modes, plus duration and resolution settings and an Advanced panel. First and last frames are kept in the permanent Asset Library.

## Where in the app
Side menu: Video > Cinema.

## Depends on
MuAPI; Asset Library.
