## What it does
Generates influencer-style content from a face and a character type (Human, Alien, Amphibian, Reptile, Mantis, Octopus and more), with format presets for Instagram Post, Story / Reel, YouTube Thumb and Pinterest Pin.

## Where in the app
Not in the side menu. Linked from the landing page header (`src/components/landing/common/Header.jsx`).

## Depends on
MuAPI.
