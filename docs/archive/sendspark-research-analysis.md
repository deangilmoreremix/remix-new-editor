## 🎯 **Sendspark vs Current Implementation**

### **Current Implementation (Text Overlay System)**
```
Base Video + Contact Data → Text Replacement → Personalized Video
```
- ✅ Simple and fast
- ✅ Works with existing video content
- ✅ Cost-effective
- ❌ Limited to text-only personalization
- ❌ Requires pre-made video templates

### **Sendspark-Style Implementation (AI Video Creation)**
```
Script + Contact Data → AI Avatar + Voice → Generated Video
```
- ✅ Creates videos from scratch
- ✅ AI-powered personalization
- ✅ No video templates needed
- ✅ More dynamic and engaging
- ❌ More complex and expensive
- ❌ Requires AI infrastructure
