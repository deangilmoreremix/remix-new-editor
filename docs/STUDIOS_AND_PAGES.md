## Notes

- Website Builder (8 features) and Video Pages (3) have no own screenshots: both need a signed-in account and Supabase settings to load. Their guides use the studio thumbnail.
- Text to Image, Image to Image, Text to Video, Image to Video, Video to Video and the Watermark Remover are launcher pages: every button opens Image, Edit or Video Studio. Smart Video Scheduler is a placeholder (its tabs show messages only).

## Not in this app

- **VidBee** and **Warmly** are separate repositories (deangilmoreremix/VidBee, deangilmoreremix/warmbly). They are not part of this app and have no guide.

## Where the content appears

- Public guide pages: /studios and /studios/<slug> (prerendered, in sitemap.xml), linked from the landing page header, app grid, Six Engines chips, scrolling strip and Feature guides section.
- Ask SmartVideo: the panel shows the current studio's guide (every feature, with screenshots), answers from the guides without an OpenAI key, and links answers to the feature's guide section. It is also on every guide page.
